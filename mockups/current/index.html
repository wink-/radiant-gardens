The process

Consult, design, build, harvest

A simple, unhurried path from "someday a garden" to your first bowl of cherry tomatoes.

  1. Consult

    We walk your yard together — sun, space, soil — and talk about how your family likes to eat.

  2. Design

    A personalized garden plan, tuned to our region and your table, which you approve before anything is built.

  3. Build

    The founder personally builds your garden in one to two focused days on site — beds, soil, irrigation, trellises.

  4. First harvest

    First-season planting help, coaching, and lifetime support as your garden starts producing.
