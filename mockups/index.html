Plain static HTML, outside the Astro build. Every mockup links the real src/styles/global.css and differs only through its local variant.css. Markup is byte-identical everywhere. See mockups/README.md for the full workflow.

Legend

  • Baseline — current/: faithful reproduction of the production homepage (hero, services overview, three differentiators, process teaser, CTA band).
  • Variant — dated folder YYYY-MM-DD-<name>/: one design variable changed versus the baseline, via tokens in variant.css only.
  • Rules: one variable at a time · identical markup · real global.css linked, never forked · real content only · verdicts recorded in docs/notes.md.
  • Promotion: an adopted variant is ported into src/styles/global.css on a design/<name> branch and merged by normal PR.
